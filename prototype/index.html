Best for polishing one screenshot at a time with precise control.

  • Annotate: arrow, rectangle, text, highlighter, blur, numbered steps, pen. Each has a one-key shortcut.
  • Backgrounds: mesh gradients, wallpapers, solids and transparent, with optional film grain.
  • Layout: padding, corner roundness, four shadow depths, and a macOS, browser or glass window frame.
  • Sizes: Auto, 16:9 for X and YouTube, 4:5 for Instagram, 1.91:1 link cards, 9:16 stories.
  • Crop: handles on every side, a rule-of-thirds grid, live pixel readout.
  • Export: PNG, JPG or WebP at 1×, 2× or 3×. Copy, save, or drag the canvas out.

No editor window. The capture overlay is the editor: select, mark up and copy without leaving the screen you're on.

Best for quick captures pasted into Slack, Linear or docs seconds later.

  • Capture modes: area, window, full screen, scrolling (stitched), and recording to MP4 or GIF, plus a 3-second timer.
  • Precise selection: a magnifier loupe with pixel coordinates, edges that snap to the window, resize from any side.
  • The toolbar follows the selection, so you draw before saving. Turn the tool off to move the selection again.
  • Live preview card: the background is applied while you're still selecting. Drag the card into any app.
  • Pin a capture as an always-on-top reference, or copy the text out of it (OCR).
  • Drag on the dimmed screen to start a new selection.

The canvas takes the whole window and the controls float over it. Backgrounds are big tiles you scan, not a dropdown.

Best for marketing shots, changelogs and social posts where the look matters most.

  • Background shelf with large previews, Shuffle, and Match shot, which picks a background from the screenshot's own colors.
  • Ambient glow: the room around the canvas takes the background's color so you judge it in context.
  • Adaptive accent: buttons and the active tool recolor to match the background. Try Peach, then Lagoon.
  • 3D tilt, film grain and a frosted glass frame.
  • A floating dock for tools and colors, a Look panel for padding, roundness, shadow and frame.
  • Social sizes in the top bar, with the export size shown live.

Every capture is kept, searchable and editable later. The editor is a side panel next to your history.

Best for people who take dozens of screenshots a day: docs, support, QA, product teams.

  • History grouped by day, with collections, pinned items and recordings.
  • Search reads the text inside images (OCR at capture time). Try "northwind".
  • Presets save a look: background, padding, frame, size. Click one in the sidebar to apply it to every selected capture.
  • Smart redact spots email addresses and updates as you crop. One click blurs them all.
  • Batch actions: export to several sizes at once, combine shots side by side, share links that expire.
  • Non-destructive: annotations stay editable, so you can fix a typo next week.
